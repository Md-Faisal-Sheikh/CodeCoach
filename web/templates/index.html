What this is

A coding-education platform built around a research question: do LLM hints help students without giving the solution away? Students submit code; a sandboxed runner grades it against hidden tests and returns a per-test verdict strip. The AI hint engine produces progressive, solution-aware-but-not-revealing hints, and an over-hinting detector measures how much each hint leaks. Instructors rate hint helpfulness and correctness against a baseline of raw compiler errors, and grade submissions by hand so autograder agreement can be measured.
